Before We Start

Terms & Conditions

Plain language. No legal jargon. Just a clear outline of how working together actually works — for your benefit and mine.

Last updated: May 2025  ·  Blue Thought Studios  ·  Operated by one person

1. Payment

For lower-cost services (photo editing, short writing, voice-over), full payment is required upfront before work begins.

For mid-range and larger projects (illustration, book covers, video editing), a 50% deposit is required upfront with the remaining 50% due upon delivery of the final file.

Work does not begin until the deposit or full payment has been received. No exceptions.

2. Delivery

Final files are only delivered after payment has been made in full. A low-resolution or watermarked preview will be provided for approval before the final file is released.

Turnaround time depends on project complexity and current workload. An estimated timeline will be discussed before the project begins but is not guaranteed unless explicitly agreed upon in writing.

3. Revisions

Every project includes 1–2 free revisions. A revision means adjusting or refining something within the original agreed scope — not changing direction entirely.

Revisions that go beyond the original scope, or requests made after the final file has been delivered, may incur additional fees. These will always be discussed before any extra work begins.

4. Usage Rights

Upon receipt of full payment, the client receives full commercial usage rights to the delivered work. This means you can use it for your project, business, or publication without restriction.

Blue Thought Studios retains the right to display the work in its portfolio and for promotional purposes unless otherwise agreed in writing before the project begins.

Ownership of the original work and all copyright remains with Blue Thought Studios unless a full copyright transfer is specifically agreed upon and reflected in the project price.

5. Cancellations

If a client cancels a project after work has begun, the deposit is non-refundable. This covers the time and work already invested.

If Blue Thought Studios is unable to complete a project for any reason, a full refund will be issued.

6. Client Responsibilities

The client is responsible for providing all necessary materials, references, and information needed to complete the project. Delays caused by late or missing information from the client may affect the timeline.

By requesting a service, the client confirms they have the rights to any materials they provide (text, images, brand assets, etc.) and that the final work will not be used for anything illegal or harmful.

7. Communication

All project communication happens via email or the contact form on this site. Response time is typically within 24–48 hours. Clear, detailed briefs lead to better work and faster delivery — the more context provided upfront, the better.

8. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated occasionally. The version in effect at the time a project is agreed upon applies to that project.

Questions about any of this? Reach out before starting a project and everything can be discussed openly. Get in touch.
